## 📖 What Is adhan-mute?

adhan-mute is a simple program that automatically lowers or mutes your computer's sound whenever it's time for Muslim prayers (adhan). It works with Spotify, YouTube, Netflix, games, or any other app playing audio. No more manually turning down the volume when prayer time arrives.

## 🧠 How Does It Work?

Your computer checks your location using your IP address to determine accurate prayer times for your city. When the adhan time comes, adhan-mute instantly silences your system audio. After the prayer window ends, your sound returns to normal automatically.
